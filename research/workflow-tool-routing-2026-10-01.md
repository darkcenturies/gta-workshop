# Public tool routing in workflows — 2026-10-01

## Question and observation

Do workflow guides route readers to the actual published Valkyrie tools?
The source catalog already linked 45 source/helper files across ten families,
but the main task and research workflows primarily described upstream references
and generic methods. They lacked a complete tool-selection and execution route.

## Change and reproduction

The [task workflow](../docs/GTA-SA-MOD-WORKFLOW.md#select-and-run-public-tools)
now routes all ten families by task, links their actual source notes and explains
`list`, `show`, runtime prerequisites and execution with supplied inputs.
The [worked routes](../docs/workshop/EXAMPLES.md) link published helpers and
three synthetic demo commands. Task-flow and evidence maps include tool selection,
execution prerequisites and actual-use records. Agent rules and the finding
template require exact entry IDs, source hashes, commands and results when used.

## Validation and limits

Inventory/source hashes, public paths, research checksums, documentation links,
rendered diagrams and the three synthetic examples were checked. No tool source
or generated research archive changed. The demos exercise collision comparison,
original content generation and synthetic coverage repeatability. Other host,
project and game-dependent routes remain conditional; this documentation change
does not establish their execution or gameplay compatibility. A listed reference
or selected family is not evidence that a tool ran.
