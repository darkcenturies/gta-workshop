# Doctor & Crashfix for GTA San Andreas

The combined `doctor-valkyrie.asi` links Doctor with the GPL-3.0 Crashfix
implementation. Distribute this combined binary under GPL-3.0 and include the
corresponding source and `../valkyrie-crashfix/LICENSE`. Doctor's original BSD-3-Clause notices and all upstream
Crashfix attribution remain applicable to their source files. The combination
does not relicense third-party resources under the repository's root license.

Doctor supplies crash reports and the attributed CrashInfo GTA SA rules.
Crashfix supplies signature-checked native guards. There are no embedded
artwork resources. Replace old Doctor and standalone Crashfix installations
with one combined ASI while the game is closed; do not run both guard copies.
Compilation and isolated tests are separate from GTA SA runtime validation.
